What Is Nightcore Music?

Nightcore is a music style that originated in Norway around 2001, created by Thomas S. Nilsen and Steinar Vadmyr. The name comes from their DJ project, and the technique quickly spread across the internet, especially through anime music videos on YouTube.

At its core, Nightcore involves speeding up a song — typically by 20–35% — which naturally raises the pitch, giving vocals a higher, euphoric quality. The result is an energetic, upbeat version of the original track that feels fresh and immersive.

Key characteristics of a Nightcore remix include:

  • Higher tempo — usually between 140–180 BPM, creating a driving, dance-friendly rhythm.
  • Raised pitch — vocals sound lighter and more ethereal, which gives the track its signature feel.
  • Emotional intensity — the combination of speed and pitch amplifies the emotion of the original song.
  • Genre flexibility — pop, EDM, rock, J-pop, and virtually any genre can be "nightcored."

Nightcore has grown into a massive community on YouTube, Spotify, and SoundCloud. Creators pair their remixes with anime-style artwork, building a distinct visual and audio aesthetic loved by millions of listeners worldwide.

Making your own Nightcore remix with Nightcore Maker is fast, free, and requires zero technical knowledge. Follow these simple steps:

Download Your Remix

Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats are supported?
Nightcore Maker currently supports .mp3 and .wav file formats. These cover the vast majority of audio files. Simply drag your file into the upload area or click to browse your device. Support for additional formats like OGG and FLAC may be added in future updates.
Is my audio uploaded to a server?
No. All audio processing is performed 100% locally inside your web browser using the Web Audio API. Your files never leave your device — nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external server. Your music stays private.
Is Nightcore Maker free to use?
Yes! The core tool — uploading audio, adjusting speed, volume, reverb, and live previewing — is completely free with no limits. Premium members (via Patreon) unlock additional features: downloading finished remixes as MP3/WAV files and the Bass Boost effect.
What is the ideal speed for a Nightcore remix?
The classic Nightcore sound typically uses a speed of 1.20x to 1.35x. The default setting in this tool is 1.25x, which works great for most songs. Feel free to experiment — some tracks sound best at higher or lower speeds depending on the original BPM and genre.
Does it work on mobile devices?
Yes. Nightcore Maker is fully responsive and works on modern mobile browsers including Chrome, Safari, and Firefox on both Android and iOS. All processing runs client-side, so no app installation is required — just open the page and start creating.
Is there a file size limit?
There is no hard file size limit imposed by the tool since everything runs in your browser. However, very large files (over ~100 MB) may cause performance issues depending on your device's available memory. For the best experience, standard-length songs (3–8 minutes) work perfectly.
What does the Reverb Decay slider do?
The Reverb Decay slider controls how much echo or "room" effect is added to the audio. At 0% you hear the dry (original) signal. Increasing it blends in a synthetic reverb that makes the track sound like it was recorded in a large hall. A subtle setting (10–25%) can add depth without muddying the mix.

Nightcore Maker is a tool — you are responsible for the content you create. If you own the original audio or have the necessary licenses, you can use the output however you wish. If you are remixing copyrighted material, the resulting track is subject to the original rights holder's terms. Always make sure you have the proper permissions before distributing or monetizing any remix.
